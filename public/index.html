About Excellentia Arts Fiesta

Excellentia Arts Fiesta 2026 is the premier inter-team cultural championship of Ma'din School of Excellence, Malappuram, celebrated under the banner "Discover the Unseen".

Featuring four prestigious teams: Bukhara (The Golden Vanguard), Undulus (The Emerald Tide), Samarkhand (The Mystic Luminary), and Qurthuba (The Crimson Horizon) across A-Zone, B-Zone, C-Zone, and General categories.

How to Use This App

  • Icon Navigation: Use the top bar icons to switch between Home, Leaderboard, Results Hub, Videos, Gallery, News, and About.
  • Live Points & Podium: Explore the real-time recalculated points matrix and podium standings in the Teams & Points section.
  • Results & Posters: Browse results by zone and click Poster ↗ on any result card to generate an instant, customizable HD graphic poster for social sharing.
  • Slideshow & Arena Mode: Open the Theater Slideshow in the Videos view for continuous broadcast of declared results and periodic team standings.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How are team points calculated?

Team scores are dynamically computed from all declared results based on standard criteria: 1st Place (10 pts), 2nd Place (7 pts), 3rd Place (5 pts), with additional grade points (Grade A: 5 pts, Grade B: 3 pts, Grade C: 1 pt).

Why are numeric points sometimes concealed?

The Fiesta Audit Committee may enable score concealment or cutoff modes during critical stages to build excitement before final grand valedictory reveals.

Can I download and share result posters?

Yes! Every result card features a Poster button allowing you to download high-resolution PNG posters in multiple artistic styles for WhatsApp status, Instagram, or prints.

Terms & Conditions (T&C)
